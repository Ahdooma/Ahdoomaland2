Lucent

Lucent or Lucent Motors is a conceptual idea for optimizing Car UI and improving modern infotainment center designs to better suit the user behind the wheel. Our focus is improving the UI for young adults ages 20-25. Creating a case study by gathering over 100 responses from individuals about what they want in an interface but also what they feel modern UIs do wrong. As a group we divided the project into 5 different sections, Gauge Cluster, Voice Assistance, Navigation, Home Screen, and Spotify/Music

Mission

A clutter free driving experience that is comfortable and intuitive. To provide essentials for all users to have an easily accessible driving experience.
